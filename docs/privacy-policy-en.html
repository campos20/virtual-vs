Virtual VS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026 · Ler em português

Virtual VS is a multitrack backing-track player for live musicians. This policy is short because the answer is short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share personal data with anyone, including the developer.

What data the app uses

The audio stems, projects, and setlists you create are saved only as local files on your own device's storage, using Expo's file system APIs. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it (for example, generating a backup file) — in that case, you pick the destination through your operating system's native share sheet (iOS/Android).

What the app does not do

  • No account, login, or user registration.
  • No server or backend of its own: the app works fully offline.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, or third-party crash reporting libraries.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifiers.

Device permissions

The app requests file access only for actions you explicitly start: importing audio stems through the system's document picker, or exporting/sharing a project backup. On Android, the app also requests a foreground service permission for media playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) so that audio keeps playing in sync while the screen is off or another app is in the foreground during a live set — this permission is used only to keep audio playing and does not collect or transmit any data.

The About screen contains a link to the project's page on GitHub. If you tap it, it opens in your device's browser, outside the app; Virtual VS itself does not send any request or data to that page or any other server.

Data deletion

Since everything is saved only on your device, deleting a project or setlist, or uninstalling the app, removes that data permanently — there is no server-side copy to delete.

Children

Virtual VS is not made exclusively for children, but its content is neutral (playing back audio tracks) and can be used by anyone, including children. Because the app does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal data from any user — adult or child — there is no collection, use, or sharing of children's data under any circumstance.

Commitment to the Google Play Families Policy

The developer has reviewed the app against the requirements of the Google Play Families Policy and commits to following it. This is consistent with the rest of this policy: because Virtual VS does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from any user, it is also compliant with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (US) and GDPR (EU), since there is no child data to protect in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with the updated date revised at the top.
